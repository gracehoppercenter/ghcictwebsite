# Final Portfolio

# Project Introduction

This project will count as your final exam, which is worth 20% of your semester 2 grade.

This year you've done a ton of great work, developed some important career skills, 
and made some projects you should be proud of! But one of the most important
career skills is the ability to communicate about the work that you've done - 
especially to non-technical audiences.

For our final project, you'll build a nice portfolio website that includes
descriptions of at least two projects you did in our class this year.

If you need inspiration, feel free to check out my personal portfolio here: <https://chrismjon.es/>

# Project Requirements

Your website should include at least three pages - an index, and two project-specific pages.

Your descriptions should be clear enough that they could be understood by a potential
employer, and should be polished enough that you would be excited to include it in
a portfolio or on your resume.

You should imagine that the person reading your report has some technical
background but does not know the specific technology you're writing about.

Your writeup should introduce the technology you studied, explain why it matters 
and how it might be used, and the specific steps that you took to explore it.

All of this information should be on the project page - you should include any 
images our outputs that you developed in your project. You should link to your github, 
and should keep any code snippets in your page to a minimum.

You likely already have some writeup in a Jupyter notebook. You're welcome to copy/paste
into your HTML page.

# Grading

I do not have a strict rubric for this project - you should make this your own,
and I care that you are proud of your work. I will give feedback as you are working.
